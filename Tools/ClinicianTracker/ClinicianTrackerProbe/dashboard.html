1 · Participant number

The participant only sees this number and selects Confirm. Set it any time before they confirm, including while you are in Researcher mode.

2 · Calibration

Normally calibrate in the headset's Researcher mode. "Calibrate with participant" is the fallback: they hold the tracker in their moving hand for ~15 s.

3 · Clinician hand

Mirrored: touching the unaffected arm appears on the affected side. Hidden until the mirror experience and clinician-touch stages.

Participant's view

Opens a live window of what the headset shows (USB, read-only, both eyes, not recorded). Close the window to stop.

Pilot session steps

  1. Researcher puts on the headset and chooses Researcher. With the tracker strapped on the hand as the clinician wears it: Calibrate tracker, move that hand slowly ~15 s, then Confirm alignment with the hand in view, then Done.
  2. Choose the participant number here and click Show on headset. Set the clinician hand to Mirrored.
  3. Strap the tracker on the clinician. Hand the headset to the participant: they choose Participant, confirm their number and choose their affected arm.
  4. Watch the Calibration card: it should stay green through the handover. If it turns amber or red, use Accept without check (logged) or Calibrate with participant.
  5. The clinician hand appears only in the mirror experience and the clinician-touch stage.
  6. At Clinician touch: stroke the unaffected forearm from elbow to wrist.
